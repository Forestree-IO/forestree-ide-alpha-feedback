# forestree-ide-alpha-feedback
Capture feedback and issue reports from alpha users of the IDE

## Thank you for trying the Forestree IDE alpha

Thank you for being one of our earliest users! Your time and feedback genuinely shape what Forestree becomes, and we're grateful you're here.

## How to give feedback

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Forestree-IO/forestree-ide-alpha-feedback/issues/new) in this repo.

You can also click the feedback icon (the speech bubble) in the IDE's bottom-left status bar — it opens this repo's Issues page.

- **Don't worry about duplicates.** If someone has already reported it, that's fine — we'll triage and link duplicates together.
- **Write as much or as little as you like.** Repro steps, screenshots, your OS and IDE version all help, but they're optional.
- **No need to format or label anything.** We'll take care of the rest.
